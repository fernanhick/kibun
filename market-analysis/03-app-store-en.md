# App Store (iOS) — English Listing (compliance-safe)

All copy below is **character-counted** against Apple's limits. Paste each block into App Store Connect exactly as written.

> **Apple ASO weighting:** Name > Subtitle > Keywords field > Description. Promotional text is **not** indexed for search.
>
> **v2 rewrite:** scrubbed of all AI claims and clinical/symptom vocabulary that previously triggered rejection under Guidelines 1.4.1 (Medical) and 4.5.5 (Misleading AI claims).

---

## 1. App Name — **30 chars max**

```
Kibun: Mood Tracker Journal
```

**[27 / 30]** ✓

- Captures both `mood tracker` and `journal` — the two highest-volume keywords in the category.
- Brand first (`Kibun:`) anchors identity over time.
- No AI / no clinical language.

### Alternatives

| Option | Chars | When to use |
|---|---|---|
| `Kibun: Mood Tracker & Diary` | 27 | If "diary" outperforms "journal" |
| `Kibun · Cute Mood Diary` | 23 | Lean harder into kawaii |
| `Kibun: Mood Journal & Habits` | 28 | Lead with habit angle |

## 7. Category & subcategory

- **Primary:** `Lifestyle`
- **Secondary:** `Health & Fitness`

> **v2 change:** flipped the primary from Health & Fitness to **Lifestyle**. This matters: positioning Kibun in Lifestyle reduces the chance Apple's reviewer flags clinical/medical claims. Daylio, Finch, and Reflectly sit in Health & Fitness, but they have well-known cleared listings. For a relaunch after rejection, Lifestyle is the safer primary.

## 8. App Privacy section (App Store Connect)

For Kibun (anonymous-first + on-device data):

- **Data Linked to You:** Email (only if signed in) · Purchase History (RevenueCat)
- **Data Not Linked to You:** Diagnostics (crash logs, anonymous), Usage Data (anonymous)
- **Data Not Collected:** Journal content, mood data (stays on device by default)

> **Don't claim** in this section anything you can't audit. Apple cross-references the description against the privacy nutrition label — inconsistency is a rejection trigger.
