# English Keyword Strategy (compliance-safe)

> **Rewrite note:** all AI keywords and clinical/symptom keywords have been removed. The kawaii + diary + privacy + habits + patterns angles still leave a strong, ranked-up strategy. Long-tail wins remain the core play.

## Strategy in one paragraph

Don't try to outrank **Daylio** for `mood tracker` — they have years of installs feeding their rank. Instead, target **mid-tail compound queries** (2-3 words, lower volume, much lower difficulty) where Kibun's *kawaii Shiba + privacy + cozy daily ritual* combo can rank top-10 in weeks, and **long-tail niche queries** (4+ words) where you can take #1 almost immediately. Compound long-tail ranks become a discovery flywheel.

## Keywords you might be tempted to add — and why they're risky

| Keyword | Why it's risky |
|---|---|
| `anxiety`, `depression`, `panic`, `stress disorder`, `ptsd`, `bipolar`, `adhd`, `ocd` | All trigger Apple's medical-claim review path (Guideline 1.4.1). Even when popular and high-converting, they can get the listing rejected. |
| `therapy`, `therapist`, `cbt`, `dbt`, `mental health`, `clinical` | Same — treating-illness framing. |
| `ai`, `ai journal`, `smart journal`, `predictive mood`, `analyzes your mood` | AI-misrepresentation review path. Even if your app technically uses on-device ML, claiming "AI" requires demonstrating the feature in the way Apple expects, or facing rejection. |
| `cures`, `heal`, `treat`, `medication`, `prescription` | Hard rejection territory. |

> If you have data that an AI or clinical keyword would 3x conversion **and** you have proper medical-app status and AI disclosures ready, that's a separate ASO program. For Kibun as a wellness/lifestyle product, the strategy above is the maximally aggressive *safe* set.

---

## What NOT to do

- ❌ Don't put `app` in your name/keywords — Apple ignores it.
- ❌ Don't put your category (`Lifestyle`, `Health & Fitness`) in keywords — Apple already indexes you under it.
- ❌ Don't duplicate words across title/subtitle/keywords.
- ❌ Don't add competitor names (`daylio`, `reflectly`, `finch`).
- ❌ Don't add any term from the **Banned vocabulary** in `README.md`.
