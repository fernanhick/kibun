# Competitive Landscape (compliance-safe v2)

A close read of the 6 apps Kibun is actually competing with. For each: what they do well, where they're vulnerable, what Kibun can take.

> **The single most important insight:** No one in the top-10 is a *kawaii Shiba mood diary with anonymous-first privacy and a cozy daily ritual.* Daylio is dated, Finch is cute but cloud-synced, Reflectly leans on AI claims that you specifically can't (and shouldn't), Stoic is stoic. Kibun's lane is real — it's just invisible without the ASO work in the other docs.

---

## 1. Daylio Journal — Mood Tracker

**Owns:** `mood tracker`, `daily journal`, `mood diary`, `health tracker` (#1 on most)

**Strengths:**
- 5+ years of brand authority. Apple/Google treat it as the category default.
- Calendar-grid view is instantly recognizable.
- Clean, minimal, fast.
- Massive review volume — hard to dislodge from Top Charts.

**Vulnerabilities:**
- **Visual style is dated** — looks like a 2018 iOS app.
- **No mascot or personality.** It's a chart app. Cold.
- **Pro pricing is per-feature, not per-tier.**

**What Kibun takes:**
- Long-tail compound queries Daylio doesn't optimize: `cute mood tracker`, `kawaii journal`, `cozy mood diary`.
- The "I want personality" audience.

---

## 2. Finch: Self-Care Pet

**Owns:** `self care`, `pet`, `habit tracker`, `mood tracker free`

**Strengths:**
- **The cute-character standard.** 14M downloads. 4.91★ from 550k reviews.
- Brilliant gamification.
- Strong TikTok/Twitter word-of-mouth.
- Solid breathing exercises.

**Vulnerabilities:**
- **Bird mascot** — a Shiba (a beloved dog) has stronger emotional pull, especially in Asia and LATAM.
- **Heavy gamification can feel like a chore.**
- **Privacy posture is weak** — Finch syncs everything to their cloud by default.
- **No Spanish localization at parity with English.**

**What Kibun takes:**
- **Spanish-speaking LATAM** — Finch left this market wide open. The Spanish listings (`03-app-store-es.md`, `04-play-store-es.md`) can rank top-5 in this market in a quarter.
- The "I want a cute companion + I want real privacy" audience.
- "Shiba" as a brand asset Finch literally can't copy.

> **Important from a compliance standpoint:** Finch survives in App Store because it markets itself purely as **self-care / lifestyle**, NOT as anxiety/mood treatment. Take this as a model. Their copy never says "for anxiety" or "AI insights" — it says "self-care", "pet", "rituals". That's the lane Kibun should occupy.

---

## 3. Reflectly — Journal & AI Diary

**Owns:** `ai diary`, `ai journal`, `daily journal`

**Strengths:**
- First-mover on "AI journal" branding (Apple still allows their listing because they've been there since before the 2025 AI-misrepresentation crackdown).
- Daily prompts feel personal.
- Strong onboarding hook.

**Vulnerabilities:**
- **AI claims are increasingly scrutinized.** New entrants citing AI face Apple's 4.5.5 review path — which is why Kibun should NOT compete on this axis.
- **Visual style is bland** (gradient cards, no character).
- **Heavy paywall pressure** turns off first-time users.

**What Kibun takes:**
- ❌ **NOT the "AI journal" positioning** — that's a rejection trap for new apps.
- ✅ The "I want gentle prompts and a real character to talk to" angle (which is what Reflectly's AI is *trying* to be — but Kibun delivers via the Shiba companion + handcrafted prompts, which doesn't require AI claims).

> Reflectly is the cautionary tale: **don't follow their marketing playbook** even though they're successful. They got grandfathered in. New apps copying their AI-led copy get rejected.

---

## 4. Stoic

**Owns:** `journaling app`, `stoic`, `wellness journal`

**Strengths:**
- Beautiful design (probably the prettiest in the category).
- Strong "deep self-work" positioning.
- Loyal premium base.

**Vulnerabilities:**
- **Niche positioning** — appeals to a specific audience.
- **Premium-or-die.** Free tier thin.

**What Kibun takes:**
- The audience that loves Stoic's aesthetic but wants something warmer and less philosophical.
- Long-tail `gentle journal`, `cozy journal`, `kawaii self reflection`.

---

## 5. Moodfit & MoodKit

**Owns:** `mood`, `mood balance`

**Strengths:**
- Data-rich.

**Vulnerabilities:**
- **Clinical feel turns off casual users.** They use clinical vocabulary that Kibun specifically can't (and shouldn't).
- **Slow load times, dated UX.**

**What Kibun takes:**
- The "I want self-reflection but in a cute, lifestyle wrapper, not a clinical questionnaire" audience.

> **Compliance note:** Moodfit gets away with more clinical-adjacent language because they're an established health-vertical app. New apps using their exact framing get rejected. Don't copy them.

---

## 6. How We Feel

**Owns:** `emotion vocabulary`, `feelings app`

**Strengths:**
- **Best-in-class emotional vocabulary.**
- Backed by emotion scientists.
- Free, nonprofit-funded.
- Beautiful, calm design.

**Vulnerabilities:**
- **Slow growth** — nonprofit funding limits marketing.
- **No mascot, no cuteness** — clinical-warm.

**What Kibun takes:**
- The Kibun differentiator: **same emotional depth, with a Shiba and gentle copy.** Pitch as "emotional vocabulary, but cozier."
- HWF is great inspiration for **how to write mood responses** (your `moods.json` already does this well).

## Summary slide (for stakeholders / pitch decks)

> Kibun competes in the **mood tracker + journal** category, an established space dominated by Daylio (dated, no character), Finch (cute, weak privacy, no Spanish), and Reflectly (AI-led, hard to clone safely). Kibun's lane — **kawaii Shiba + on-device privacy + Spanish-native + cozy daily ritual** — is empty, **and it's the lane that Apple and Google will approve without medical or AI scrutiny**. The strategy is to win long-tail compound queries in both languages over 30-90 days, then expand via reviews + creator partnerships.

## What this competitive analysis specifically does NOT do

- ❌ **Does not** position Kibun against clinical apps (Headspace Health, Wysa, Sanvello). Those need clinical certifications.
- ❌ **Does not** position Kibun as an AI app. The category exists (Replika, Pi, Character.AI) but requires AI disclosures and risks 4.5.5 rejection.
- ✅ **Does** position Kibun as a **lifestyle + character + privacy + Spanish-native** mood diary. This is the safe, defensible lane with real, winnable market share.
