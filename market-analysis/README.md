# Kibun — Market Analysis & ASO Strategy (v2 — compliance-safe)

Generated 2026-05-21 · For **App Store (iOS)** and **Google Play (Android)** · **English + Spanish**.

> **⚠️ Compliance rewrite.** A prior submission of Kibun was rejected for content that triggered Apple's medical-claim and AI-misrepresentation review pathways. This entire analysis has been reworked to be **review-safe**: zero mentions of AI / IA / artificial intelligence / smart / machine learning, and zero clinical or symptom vocabulary (anxiety, depression, panic, therapy, CBT, mental health, treatment, diagnosis). See "Banned vocabulary" below for the full list.

---

## TL;DR — Why downloads are flat

Kibun is a polished kawaii Shiba mood-tracker + mini-journal, but the store listing isn't doing its job.

1. **The app name ("Kibun") carries no search intent.** It's a Japanese word — nobody types it. The current title `Kibun` gives Apple/Google zero keywords to rank you for.
2. **No keyword-loaded subtitle / short description.** The single biggest ASO lever you're leaving on the table.
3. **Screenshots probably don't sell the outcome in the first 2-3 frames** (70-80% of install decisions happen there).
4. **No long-tail strategy.** "Mood tracker" is locked by Daylio. You can't beat them head-on — you have to win mid-tail compound queries like *"cute mood tracker"*, *"kawaii mood journal"*, *"private mood diary"*.

This folder gives you the **exact strings to paste**, the **screenshot script to shoot**, and a **30-day rollout plan** — all guideline-safe.

## 🚫 Banned vocabulary (do NOT use anywhere)

### AI / tech-magic terms (Apple AI-misrepresentation flag)
`AI`, `IA`, `artificial intelligence`, `inteligencia artificial`, `machine learning`, `ML`, `neural`, `algorithm`, `smart` *(in tech sense)*, `intelligent`, `automatic analysis`, `predictive`, `learns from you`, `understands you`, `chatbot`, `assistant` *(in AI sense)*, `GPT`, `LLM`

### Clinical / medical terms (Apple Guideline 1.4.1)
`anxiety`, `depression`, `panic`, `panic attack`, `bipolar`, `PTSD`, `OCD`, `ADHD`, `disorder`, `condition`, `symptom`, `diagnose`, `diagnosis`, `treatment`, `treat`, `therapy`, `therapeutic`, `therapist`, `CBT`, `clinical`, `mental health` *(use "emotional wellness" instead)*, `mental illness`, `psychiatric`, `medical`, `medication`, `cure`, `healing` *(in clinical sense)*, `recovery`, `trauma`

### Spanish equivalents to avoid
`ansiedad`, `depresión`, `pánico`, `trastorno`, `enfermedad`, `tratar`, `tratamiento`, `terapia`, `terapéutico`, `clínico`, `salud mental` *(usa "bienestar emocional")*, `diagnosticar`, `psiquiátrico`, `médico`, `medicación`, `cura`, `curar`, `sanar` *(en sentido clínico)*, `recuperación`, `trauma`, `TDAH`, `TOC`

### Borderline — use sparingly with care
`stress` / `estrés` — usually OK as a lifestyle term, but pair with non-clinical context ("daily stress" yes, "stress disorder" no)
`wellness` / `bienestar` — fine in lifestyle, avoid pairing with "mental"
`sleep` / `sueño` — fine as habit input, avoid "sleep disorder"
`focus` / `enfoque` — fine in productivity sense
`mindfulness` — fine (secular wellness term)
`meditation` / `meditación` — fine (lifestyle category)
`breathing` / `respiración` — fine (described as exercise, not therapy)

## Character-limit cheat sheet (2026)

### Apple App Store
| Field | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| App name | **30** chars | Most ASO weight. |
| Subtitle | **30** chars | Second-most ASO weight. |
| Promotional text | **170** chars | Not indexed but shown above description. Update freely. |
| Description | **4,000** chars | First ~3 lines visible above "more". |
| Keywords field | **100** chars | Comma-separated, **no spaces after commas**. |

### Google Play
| Field | Limit | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| App title | **30** chars | Most ASO weight. |
| Short description | **80** chars | Above-the-fold. Heavily indexed. |
| Full description | **4,000** chars | Indexed. Keyword repetition matters (2-5×). |
| Feature graphic | **1024 × 500** px | Top of listing. Required. |

### Screenshot sizes
| Store | Required size (2026) | How many |
|---|---|---|
| iOS (primary) | **1320 × 2868** (6.9" iPhone) — Apple auto-downscales | up to 10 |
| iOS (alt) | 1290 × 2796 (6.7") or 1242 × 2688 (6.5") still accepted | up to 10 |
| Google Play (phone) | **1080 × 1920** or higher, 9:16 portrait | 2–8 |
| Google Play (feature graphic) | **1024 × 500** | 1 required |

## The single biggest move

If you ship nothing else this month, **rename the app** in both stores from `Kibun` to:

- **iOS name:** `Kibun: Mood Tracker Journal`
- **Play title:** `Kibun: Cute Mood Tracker Diary`

That one change will start showing you up for "mood tracker", "journal", and "diary" within ~5-10 days of approval. Everything else compounds on top of that — and none of it crosses the AI or clinical compliance lines that got the previous submission rejected.

## Why this version should pass review

| Apple/Google concern | What we removed |
|---|---|
| Misrepresenting AI features | All `AI`, `smart`, `intelligent`, `analyzes` language |
| Practicing medicine without authority | All clinical conditions (anxiety, depression, panic, CBT, therapy) |
| Diagnosing or treating | All "for [condition]" framing |
| Acting as a medical device | All "improves your mental health" framing |

What remains is **product description in plain lifestyle language**: a cute Shiba, a daily check-in, a private journal, breathing exercises, see-your-patterns feature — all of which describe what the app *does*, not what it *claims to cure*.
