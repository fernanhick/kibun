Frequently Asked Questions

How do I log a mood?

On the Home tab, tap the slot you want (morning, afternoon, night, or pre-sleep), pick one of the 14 mood bubbles, and optionally add a short note. That's it — the whole flow is designed to take under 10 seconds.

Do I need an account to use Kibun?

No. Kibun is anonymous-first — you can use the entire app without creating an account, and your data stays on your device only. Creating an account adds cloud sync across devices and unlocks AI Pro reports if you subscribe.

How do I start the 7-day free trial of Kibun Pro?

Open the Account tab and tap "Upgrade to Pro", or visit the paywall during onboarding. Choose monthly or yearly, then confirm with Face ID / Touch ID. Apple charges nothing for the first 7 days. Cancel anytime in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions to avoid being charged.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not Kibun. On your iPhone or iPad:

  • Open Settings
  • Tap your Apple ID at the top
  • Tap Subscriptions
  • Find Kibun Pro and tap Cancel Subscription

Cancelling at least 24 hours before the end of your trial or current period prevents the next charge. You keep Pro features until the end of the period you've already paid for.

How do I restore a previous purchase?

Open the Account tab and tap "Restore Purchases". Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID that originally purchased Pro.

How do AI reports work? Is my data private?

Kibun Pro generates weekly and monthly reports that summarize your emotional patterns. Only aggregated metadata — mood labels, counts, time-of-day buckets and your onboarding profile — is sent to a secure AI endpoint. Your raw notes and journal entries are never uploaded; sentiment for notes is analysed locally on your device using a bundled ONNX model.

For full details see our Privacy Policy.

How do I change notification reminder times?

Open the Account tab → Notifications. Toggle each slot (morning, afternoon, night, pre-sleep) on or off. Kibun Pro subscribers can also set exact times per slot; free users get sensible defaults.

I'm not getting notifications. What should I check?
  • iOS Settings → Notifications → Kibun → make sure "Allow Notifications" is on.
  • Inside Kibun, open Account → Notifications and confirm at least one slot is enabled.
  • Check that Focus / Do Not Disturb isn't filtering Kibun out.
  • If you recently reinstalled, you may need to grant the permission again.
How do I export my mood history?

Kibun Pro subscribers can export their full mood history as a CSV file. Open Account → Export Data and pick a destination (email, Files, AirDrop).

How do I delete my account or data?

You can delete your account or just your data from inside the app: Account → Danger Zone → "Delete Account" or "Delete My Data".

You can also start the deletion flow from the web at our Delete Account page.

Can I switch between monthly and yearly plans?

Yes — both plans live in the same subscription group, so you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from iOS Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → Kibun Pro. Apple handles the proration automatically.

Is Kibun a medical or therapy app?

No. Kibun is a wellness and self-reflection tool. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, or clinical advice. If you are struggling, please reach out to a licensed professional. In a crisis, contact your local emergency services or a crisis hotline.

Which devices does Kibun support?

Kibun runs on iPhone (iOS 15.1+) and iPad (iPadOS 15.1+). It also runs on Apple Silicon Macs through "Designed for iPad". An Android version is on our roadmap.

I found a bug or have a feature request.
