# Screenshot Strategy — Kibun (compliance-safe)

**The brutal truth:** 70-80% of install decisions are made from the first 2-3 screenshots. People scroll past everything else. Frames 1, 2, and 3 do all the work.

> **Rewrite note:** all AI claims and clinical/symptom framing were removed from screenshot copy. The screens now lead with **product action** ("see your patterns") rather than tech claims ("AI sees your patterns"). Same product, review-safe copy.

This document gives you the **shoot script**: every frame, every headline, in EN + ES, with character-counted copy and design rules.

| iOS (acceptable alt) | 1290 × 2796 (6.7") or 1242 × 2688 (6.5") | up to 10 |

| Google Play (feature graphic) | **1024 × 500 px** | 1 required |
| Google Play (tablet 7") | 1024 × 600 minimum | optional, ship 4-6 |

**Design system to reuse across all 8 frames:**
- Background: brand gradient (`#4A86FF` blue → teal). Vertical or diagonal.
- Headline font: bold, ~110-140pt, white with subtle shadow.
- Sub-headline: ~60-70pt, white at 80% opacity.
- Device frame: iPhone 17 mockup OR no frame (edge-to-edge). Pick one, stick with it.
- **Shiba mascot in every screenshot.** This is your strongest brand asset.
- Safe zone: keep important text inside the inner 75% of canvas.

## The "first 3" rule — non-negotiable

Apple shows the first **2** screenshots in search results. Google Play shows the first **3**. If those frames don't make the case, you lose the install. Treat the first 3 as a complete pitch:

1. **Hook** — "What is this app and why should I care?"
2. **Payoff** — "What's the magical outcome?"
3. **Feeling** — "Will this fit into my life emotionally?"

## A/B testing plan

Both stores let you run side-by-side experiments. **Always test screenshot 1 first.**

### Test 1 (14 days)
- **Control:** "Track your mood in 10 seconds" + Shiba reading
- **Variant A:** "Your Shiba is here for you" + Shiba waving (more emotional, less feature-led)
- **Variant B:** "Why did Monday feel heavy?" + Shiba with insight chart (curiosity hook, **no AI mention**)

### Test 2 (after Test 1 winner)
- Test screenshot 2: current "See what shifts how you feel" vs an emotional variant "See yourself clearly."

### Test 3
- Test feature graphic (Shiba-forward vs UI-forward).

**Rule:** Change one element at a time. Run ≥ 14 days each. Need ≥ 10k impressions per variant for significance.

## Localization rules (ES)

- **Don't shrink text size for Spanish.** Spanish runs ~25% longer; build templates with 30% extra horizontal space.
- **Use "tú / tus" (informal), not "usted / sus".** Kibun's tone is warm — formal "usted" kills the kawaii vibe.
- **Avoid Iberian-only slang** — Kibun's biggest Spanish market is LATAM. No "súper", "guay", "molar".
- **Use accents correctly** — `ánimo`, `también`, `tú`, `está`.
- **Numbers stay as digits** — "10 seconds" / "10 segundos".

## Acceptance checklist before publishing

For each of the 8 screenshots:

- [ ] Headline ≤ 5 words
- [ ] Text readable as a thumbnail (zoom to 25% to test)
- [ ] Shiba mascot present
- [ ] Brand gradient background
- [ ] Important text inside inner 75% safe zone
- [ ] No personal data / no visible email addresses in UI
- [ ] No mention of AI, smart, intelligent, analyzes
- [ ] No mention of anxiety, depression, panic, therapy, mental health, CBT
- [ ] Spanish version reviewed by a native LATAM speaker
- [ ] First 3 screenshots stand alone as a complete pitch
