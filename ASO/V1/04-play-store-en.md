# Google Play — English Listing (compliance-safe)

> **Google Play ASO weighting:** Title >>> Short description >> Full description. Google indexes the full description and rewards **keyword repetition** more than Apple (2-5× per primary keyword is the sweet spot).
>
> **v2 rewrite:** all AI and clinical/symptom vocabulary removed. Google Play also enforces "Inappropriate Content — Sensitive Events" and "Health Content" policies — both of which can be triggered by clinical claims or unverified AI claims.
