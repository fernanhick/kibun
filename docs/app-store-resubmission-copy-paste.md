Kibun is a private mood tracker and diary built for fast, low-friction daily check-ins.

Log how you feel in seconds, up to four times a day, then revisit your mood history, patterns, and streaks whenever you want. A gentle Shiba Inu mascot adds personality to every check-in so the experience feels warm, friendly, and easy to keep using.

- Calendar history shows your month at a glance
- Streaks, charts, and summary cards make progress easy to see
- Works offline so your data stays on your device unless you choose to sign in
- Private on-device processing keeps notes local
