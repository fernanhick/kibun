## Expanding to new languages (future — not part of V4)

"Every language" today = **English + Spanish**, because those are the only locales the app is localized in. To add a *new* store language responsibly you must first localize the **app UI** (`src/i18n/locales/<lang>`), not just the listing — Play penalizes (and users bounce from) a listing whose language the app doesn't speak. High-value next locales for this category, in rough priority: **Portuguese (Brazil)**, **German**, **French**, **Japanese** (strong kawaii/mood-app market + on-brand with 気分). When you're ready to localize the app into any of these, spin up a `V5` listing pass and I'll produce the store copy + keyword field for each.
